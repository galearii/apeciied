# APEC IIED website

The public website of the APEC Institute of Innovation & Education Development (APEC IIED), the institute's record of its newsletters, plans, events and people.

## Language

### Newsletters

**Newsletter issue**:
One numbered edition of the institute's newsletter, published as a PDF with a cover and a list of the topics it covers.
_Avoid_: Newsletter (for a single edition), edition, volume

### Schedule

**Schedule**:
The institute's month-by-month plan for one calendar year, covering its own work and that of its affiliated organizations.
_Avoid_: Calendar, timeline, project schedule (for the data)

**Schedule entry**:
One item on the Schedule: something happening in a given month, sometimes on a given day or span of days.
_Avoid_: Event (an APEC event is something else), item, task

**Schedule category**:
The kind of a Schedule entry: MOE projects, APEC projects, submission deadlines, meetings & workshops, or holidays.
_Avoid_: Type, tag, colour

### Events

**APEC event**:
An event the institute hosted under APEC, such as a forum, workshop or training, listed with its dates, funding and co-sponsoring economies.
_Avoid_: Project (a Project is something else), activity

**Event highlight**:
A recorded video of an APEC event the institute hosted.
_Avoid_: Video, highlight reel, recap

### Organization

**Department**:
A department of Taiwan's Ministry of Education under which one of the institute's Projects sits.
_Avoid_: Division, branch, office

**Project**:
A programme the institute runs under a Department, such as HRDWG-EDNET.
_Avoid_: Program, initiative, working group (unless it is the Project's own name)

**Leadership**:
The institute's director and deputy directors.
_Avoid_: Management, executives, board

**Team**:
The institute's staff outside Leadership.
_Avoid_: Staff, members, employees
