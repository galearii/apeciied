## APEC HRDWG-EDNET?

The APEC Human Resource Development Working Group Education Network (APEC HRDWG-EDNET) has three main objectives:

- Develop 21st Century Knowledge and Skills for All

- Integrate HRD into the global economy

- Address the Social Dimension of Globalization

Learn more about how the working group is organized to achieve results.
